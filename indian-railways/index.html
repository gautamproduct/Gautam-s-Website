2023–25 · Dedicated Freight Corridor

Station Master, Indian Railways

From September 2023 to January 2025 I worked as a Station Master at one of India's largest freight junctions on the Dedicated Freight Corridor.

Scale

~2,400 freight train operations a month, coordinated across 5+ departments, with zero room for the wheel to stop.

What it taught me about product

Operations at real scale, stakeholder management under pressure, and making decisions with incomplete information are skills I use every day as a Product Manager at PhysicsWallah.
