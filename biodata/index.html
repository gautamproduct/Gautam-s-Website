A little about me

मेरे बारे में

Namaste! 🙏 I'm Gautam. I was born in Patna, Bihar and grew up in Gujarat, spending a few years in Bharuch before my family settled in Vadodara.

I'm a BBA graduate and currently work as a Product Manager in the AI space at PhysicsWallah, an IPO-listed company in Noida, in a hybrid setup. I started my career with an Indian Railways PSU in a government role and, after 1.5 years, moved to the corporate world to pursue my interest in technology, AI & business.

My father is a government servant, my mother is a homemaker, and my brother is an IIT Kanpur graduate currently teaching at a reputed coaching institute in Hyderabad.

Outside work, I enjoy long drives, exploring new places, watching movies and reading about business & technology.

— Gautam
