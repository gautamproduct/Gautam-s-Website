FAQ

Can these case studies be used in a classroom or product club?

Yes. Each one is written from first-hand product work with the numbers that matter. Educators, product clubs and student communities are welcome to discuss them; for a walkthrough with Gautam, email gautamprep@gmail.com.

Who is Gautam Kumar?

Gautam Kumar is a Product Manager at PhysicsWallah (PW). He took Pi Lens App from 0 to 500K downloads, drives product-led growth for PW Test Series, led PW's ₹320 Cr IITJEE category, scaled MARKS at MathonGo and was a Station Master at Indian Railways.

Did Gautam do an MBA?

No. He received an MBA offer from IIM Sambalpur and a PGP offer from Mesa School of Business and chose to keep building products, a choice he talks about openly with students weighing the same decision.

For a product club, classroom or student community conversation: gautamprep@gmail.com
