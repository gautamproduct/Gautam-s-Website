Pi Lens: 0 → 400K downloads

Pi Lens is India's first YouTube-layer learning app from PhysicsWallah. It sits next to any educational YouTube video and adds notes, previous-year questions (PYQs), quizzes, polls and AI answers, on Android and as a Chrome extension. I'm the Product Manager.

Results

  • 0 → 400K+ downloads on Android and Chrome.
  • Play Store rating 4.3★ → 4.7★.
  • Day-7 retention lift through an onboarding rebuild.
  • AI answer accuracy improved from 75%.

What I owned

Every feature, release and metric: roadmap and prioritisation, AI quality, onboarding and retention, Play Store and Chrome Web Store growth, and launch content. I script, record and present the Pi Lens launch and how-to videos on YouTube myself. Brain Games, a prototype I vibe-coded, made it onto the Pi Lens roadmap.
