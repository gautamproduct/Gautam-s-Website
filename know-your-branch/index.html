Know Your Branch podcast

Know Your Branch is a MARKS podcast where IIT students say what their branch is really like, made for JEE aspirants choosing one. I was the host and ran it end to end, from bringing guests on board to bringing users to the app.

Episodes

Computer Science at IIT Kharagpur, Mechanical Engineering at IIT Delhi, Biological Engineering at IIT Madras, Civil Engineering at IIT Kharagpur, Chemical Engineering at IIT Gandhinagar and IIT Guwahati, and Pharmaceutics Engineering at IIT BHU.

Why it mattered

The series helped make MARKS YouTube the app's biggest acquisition channel, which reached 52K+ subscribers and 15.4M views.
