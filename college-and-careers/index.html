College and Careers, by Gautam Kumar

I founded and host College and Careers (@collegeandcareers), a YouTube channel and podcast for Class 9, 10, 11, 12 and dropper students who don't just chase a college, they chase meaning. We help you pick colleges and careers across domains: engineering, medical, management, commerce, law, design, humanities and more, beyond cutoffs.

What the channel covers

  • Careers across domains: engineering, medical, management, commerce, law, design, humanities and more, so students see every path, not just one exam.
  • Class 9–12 & droppers: choosing a stream after 10th, planning Class 11–12, and making a drop year count.
  • Career guidance after 12th: what to pick after 12th Science, and whether engineering is right for you.
  • Counselling and cutoffs: JoSAA and CSAB cutoffs for NITs and IIITs, explained rank by rank.
  • College reviews: IIM Bangalore's UG program, BITS Pilani, Mesa School of Business and more, beyond the brochure.
  • The podcast: honest conversations with students and graduates about colleges, branches and careers.

Every year students make one of the biggest choices of their lives from cutoffs, rankings and what relatives say.
